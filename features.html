FAQs

Everything about Chrono, Echo, and plans.

What's Chrono vs Echo?

Chrono = Booking & schedule (ranked slots, <1s). Echo = English TTS/STT in chat. Both live 24/7 with NOVA AI. Voice Channel = phone + widget mic, Multilanguage = 100+ auto.

What's in Launch?

Guide, Tracker, Widget, Chat, Knowledge, Memory, Behaviour, Analytics, Audit, Integrations, Conversations, Customers — no Chrono/Echo. Add them as add-ons or upgrade.

What's in Growth?

Launch + Chrono + Echo English — no Voice, no Multilanguage, no custom rules. Scale adds all add-ons. Custom is bespoke RLHF.

Do I need Voice or Multilanguage?

Voice Channel = phone calls + widget mic handoff. Multilanguage = auto-detect 100+ langs for STT/TTS/chat. Echo alone is English chat only.

How does yearly 20% off work?

Toggle to Yearly → top shows yearly total (e.g., $758/yr), below shows per month after 20% off ($63.20/mo) + save. Custom stays Custom.

What if I need custom rules?

Launch/Growth 0 rules, Scale 10, Custom ∞. Or buy Custom Pack +5 rules $15 add-on for any plan.
