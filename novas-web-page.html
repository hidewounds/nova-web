FAQ

Fair questions.

Will it make things up about my products?

No. This was our #1 design rule. Every reply passes a grounding guard against your knowledge base — if the answer isn't there, NOVA says it doesn't know and offers to loop in a human. No invented prices, specs or delivery dates.

Do the follow-up emails come from "noreply@robot.ai"?

The opposite. NOVA owns no email identity — every message goes out through YOUR SMTP credentials, from YOUR address, signed as your team. Customers only ever hear from you.

What does my customers' data go through?

NOVA is local-first: it can run entirely on your own server, so conversations never have to leave your infrastructure. And you can permanently erase any customer's data with one click in the portal.

How technical is setup really?

You paste one line of code. Our team configures the knowledge base, picks the role with you, and reviews the launch. Most sites are live the same day.

What if my customers hate chat widgets?

NOVA waits politely by default. Proactive engagement is off unless you enable it, and even then it fires at most once per visit — a gentle nudge, never spam.

Can it book appointments or qualify leads too?

Yes — switch its role in the portal. Same engine, different objective and rules: booking collection flows, lead qualification questions, product advising and more.
