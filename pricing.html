FAQ

Fair questions.

Will it hallucinate?

No — unified brain is still grounded in your knowledge. If not there → says so + offers human handoff. Reward optimizes for accuracy, not role. Custom behaviours never override grounding.

What happened to roles?

Deleted. Server/src/core/config/roles.js and prompt.js removed. One brain learns 6 patterns (support, sales, shopping, advisor, lead, general) via RLHF and activates them situationally without labels.

What is Custom Agent Behaviour?

A per-business builder in the dashboard. You define: trigger keywords → primary pattern + tone + instructions + priority. Example: trigger refund → support pattern, empathetic tone, boost 1.8×, priority 9. The brain merges your rule with learned weights fluidly — you steer without prompt engineering. Limits: Launch 0 (add-on $15 for 5), Growth 3, Scale 10, Unlimited ∞.

Do I need to reconfigure?

No. Existing businesses were migrated to unified automatically. Your plan now gives full pattern access + custom builder (Growth+). Launch can enable via add-on.

Real actions?

Yes — availability, bookings (confirm before), leads — all via business-level gating, not role gating. Custom behaviours can also set preferred pattern for action contexts.

Can I A/B test behaviours?

On Scale you can create multiple rules with different priorities and watch analytics; on Unlimited we can also run custom RLHF training on your own conversations to bake the behaviour into the model.
